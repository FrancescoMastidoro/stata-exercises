## Background
The NLSY80 dataset is a 1980 cross-section of 935 individuals with information on wages, education, experience, and demographic characteristics — a classic setup for human capital research in labor economics. Understanding how to declare panel structure and compare pooled OLS, between-effects, and fixed-effects estimators is essential for identifying causal returns to education and experience while controlling for unobserved heterogeneity.

## Your tasks
