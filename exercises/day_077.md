## Background
The Mroz 1987 PSID dataset is a classic benchmark in labor economics, containing detailed information on married women's labor force participation, wages, hours worked, and household characteristics. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential when a key regressor is endogenous — for example, when education and wages are jointly determined by unobserved ability — and valid instruments allow researchers to recover consistent causal estimates.

## Your tasks

1. **OLS baseline** — Load the dataset and run an OLS regression of `wwage76` (wife's hourly wage in 1976) on `wedyrs` (wife's years of education), `wexper` (wife's work experience), and `wifeage`. Report the coefficient on `wedyrs` and note its interpretation.

2. **Check instrument candidates** — The variables `wmedyrs` (wife's mother's education) and `wfedyrs` (wife's father's education) are candidate instruments for `wedyrs`. Regress `wedyrs` on both candidate instruments plus the controls `wexper` and `wifeage` to assess instrument relevance. Check the F-statistic on the excluded instruments using `test wmedyrs wfedyrs` after the regression.

3. **IV estimation with `ivregress`** — Run a 2SLS regression of `wwage76` on `wedyrs`, `wexper`, and `wifeage`, instrumenting `wedyrs` with `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs`. Use the `ivregress 2sls` command with the standard IV syntax. Compare the coefficient on `wedyrs` to your OLS estimate from Task 1.

4. **Test for endogeneity** — After your `ivregress` command, use `estat endogenous` to formally test whether `wedyrs` is endogenous. Report the test statistic and p-value, and state in a comment whether OLS or IV is preferred based on the result.

5. *(Bonus)* **Overidentification test** — Since you have two instruments for one endogenous variable, the model is overidentified. Run `estat overid` after your `ivregress` command to perform a Sargan–Hansen test of overidentifying restrictions. Interpret the result in a comment: does the test cast doubt on instrument validity?

- **Hint 1 (Task 3)**: The syntax for `ivregress 2sls` separates exogenous controls and instruments inside parentheses: `ivregress 2sls depvar (endogenous = instruments) controls`. The endogenous regressor goes on the left of the `=` inside the parentheses, and the instruments go on the right.

- **Hint 2 (Task 4)**: `estat endogenous` and `estat overid` are post-estimation commands that only work immediately after `ivregress`. Make sure you have not run any other estimation command between `ivregress` and these tests. The null hypothesis for `estat endogenous` is that the variable is *exogenous* — a small p-value suggests endogeneity is a real concern.
