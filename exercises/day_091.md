## Background
The Mroz (1987) dataset contains information on 753 married women drawn from the PSID, covering labor supply decisions, wages, family characteristics, and tax variables. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential in labor economics whenever a regressor — such as education or experience — is plausibly correlated with unobservable determinants of the outcome, making OLS estimates inconsistent.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the core variables** — Load the dataset and run `summarize` on `wwage76`, `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, `k618`, and `smsa`. Report the mean and standard deviation of each. Then check how many women have `wwage76 == 0` (non-workers): what fraction of the sample is that?

2. **Run a naive OLS regression** — Regress `wwage76` (wife's 1976 hourly wage) on `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, `k618`, and `smsa` using all 753 observations. Note the coefficient on `wedyrs` and its standard error.

3. **Assess instrument relevance** — Wives' education (`wedyrs`) may be endogenous due to ability bias. Propose `wmedyrs` (wife's mother's education) and `wfedyrs` (wife's father's education) as instruments. Run the **first-stage regression** of `wedyrs` on `wmedyrs`, `wfedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, `k618`, and `smsa`. Report the F-statistic for joint significance of the two instruments and comment on whether the relevance condition appears satisfied (a common rule of thumb is F > 10).

4. **Estimate the IV model and compare** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to instrument `wedyrs` with `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs`, keeping the same controls and outcome as in Task 2. Compare the IV coefficient on `wedyrs` to the OLS estimate: does the direction and magnitude of the change make economic sense given potential ability bias? Then run the Durbin–Wu–Hausman endogeneity test using the appropriate post-estimation command.

5. *(Bonus)* **Test overidentifying restrictions** — After the `ivregress` command, use `estat overid` to run a Sargan–Hansen test of overidentifying restrictions. State the null hypothesis of this test, report the p-value, and discuss what it implies about the joint validity of `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs` as instruments.
