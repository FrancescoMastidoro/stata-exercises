## Background
The `laborsup` dataset contains 500 observations on female labor supply, including women's and men's education, household income sources, number of children, and whether the woman works. Instrumental variables estimation is central to labor economics because key regressors like education or income are almost always endogenous — a valid instrument allows you to recover a causal effect by exploiting exogenous variation.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the data** — Load the dataset and run `describe` and `summarize`. Identify which variable measures whether the woman works (`fem_work`), which measures her income (`fem_inc`), and which could plausibly serve as an instrument for `fem_inc` (think about the husband's characteristics that affect household income but may not directly affect the wife's work decision independently of income).

2. **OLS baseline** — Regress `fem_work` on `fem_inc`, `kids`, and `fem_educ` using OLS (`regress`). Record the coefficient on `fem_inc` and note its sign and magnitude. This is your benchmark, though it may be biased if `fem_inc` is endogenous.

3. **First-stage regression** — Run a first-stage regression of `fem_inc` on your proposed instrument (`male_educ`), plus the controls `kids` and `fem_educ`. Check whether `male_educ` is a strong instrument by examining the F-statistic for the excluded instrument (use `testparm male_educ` after the regression, or inspect the overall model F). A weak instrument has a first-stage F below 10.

4. **IV estimation with `ivregress`** — Estimate the structural equation for `fem_work` using two-stage least squares (`ivregress 2sls`), instrumenting `fem_inc` with `male_educ` and controlling for `kids` and `fem_educ`. Compare the IV coefficient on `fem_inc` to your OLS estimate from Task 2 — does the direction or magnitude change meaningfully?

5. *(Bonus)* **Overidentification and robustness** — Add `other_inc` as a second instrument alongside `male_educ`. Re-run the IV regression with both instruments. Then perform the Sargan–Hansen overidentification test using `estat overid` after the regression. Interpret the p-value: a rejection suggests at least one instrument may not be excludable. Also run `ivregress liml` with both instruments and compare the LIML and 2SLS coefficients on `fem_inc` — do they differ substantially?
