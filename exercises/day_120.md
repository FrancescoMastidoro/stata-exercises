## Background
The Mroz (1987) dataset contains information on married women's labor supply decisions, wages, and family characteristics from the PSID — a classic benchmark for labor economics research. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential here because a wife's observed wage is likely endogenous to her hours worked, and credible instruments allow us to isolate exogenous variation in wages.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the outcome and endogenous variable** — Load the dataset and summarize `whrs75` (wife's hours worked) and `wwage75` (wife's hourly wage in 1975). Report the mean, standard deviation, and the share of observations where `wwage75` equals zero. Note: this variable has no missing values, but zero wages are substantively important.

2. **OLS benchmark** — Regress `whrs75` on `wwage75`, `wedyrs`, `kl6`, `k618`, and `wifeage`. Report the coefficient on `wwage75` and briefly interpret its sign and magnitude.

3. **First-stage regression** — Run a regression of `wwage75` on your proposed instrument `hedyrs` (husband's education in years) and the same controls from Task 2 (`wedyrs`, `kl6`, `k618`, `wifeage`). Report the coefficient and t-statistic on `hedyrs` and assess whether it appears to be a relevant instrument (think about the rule-of-thumb F-statistic threshold).

4. **IV estimation with `ivregress`** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to estimate the effect of `wwage75` on `whrs75`, instrumenting `wwage75` with `hedyrs` and using the same controls. Compare the IV coefficient on `wwage75` to the OLS estimate from Task 2 and discuss what the difference suggests about the direction of endogeneity bias.

5. *(Bonus)* **Weak instrument test and overidentification** — Add a second instrument `hwage75` (husband's hourly wage) to your instrument set and re-run `ivregress 2sls`. After estimation, run `estat firststage` to retrieve first-stage F-statistics and `estat overid` to perform a Sargan–Hansen overidentification test. Interpret both diagnostics: is the instrument set strong, and does it pass the overidentification test?

## Hints

- **Hint 1 (Task 1)**: After `summarize wwage75`, you can count zero-wage observations with `count if wwage75 == 0` and divide by `_N` to get the share — or use `tabstat` with the `by()` option on a generated indicator.
- **Hint 2 (Task 3)**: A commonly cited rule of thumb for instrument relevance is a first-stage F-statistic above 10. You can retrieve this after `regress` by typing `test hedyrs` to get a partial F-test, or note the model F from the output when `hedyrs` is the only excluded instrument.
- **Hint 3 (Task 4)**: The syntax for `ivregress 2sls` follows the pattern `ivregress 2sls depvar controls (endogvar = instruments)`. Make sure your controls appear *outside* the parentheses and your instrument appears *inside*.
- **Hint 4 (Bonus)**: `estat firststage` and `estat overid` must be called immediately after `ivregress` — they use results stored in memory by the most recent IV estimation. The overidentification test is only available when you have more instruments than endogenous regressors.
