## Background
The PSID extract contains 595 individuals observed over 7 time periods, with information on wages, union membership, occupation, and demographic characteristics. Difference-in-differences (DiD) is a cornerstone identification strategy in labor economics, exploiting variation in treatment timing across units to estimate causal effects while controlling for unobserved time-invariant heterogeneity.

## Your tasks

1. **Load and explore** — Load the dataset with `webuse psidextract`. Declare the panel structure using `xtset`, then use `xtdescribe` to confirm the panel is balanced. How many individuals and time periods are there?

2. **Summarize the treatment group** — Union membership (`union`) is your treatment variable. Use `tabulate union t` to describe how union membership varies across the 7 time periods. Then compute the mean of `lwage` separately for union and non-union workers in period 1 (`t == 1`) and period 7 (`t == 7`).

3. **Construct a DiD by hand** — Define a "post" indicator equal to 1 for periods `t >= 4` and 0 otherwise. Then compute the four cell means of `lwage` needed for a manual DiD calculation: union × pre, union × post, non-union × pre, non-union × post. Calculate the DiD estimate by hand from these four means.

4. **Estimate DiD via regression** — Run a regression of `lwage` on `union`, your `post` indicator, their interaction (`union × post`), and the control variables `ed`, `blk`, `fem`, and `south`. Interpret the coefficient on the interaction term. Then re-estimate the same model adding individual fixed effects with `xtreg, fe` — does the interaction coefficient change, and why?

5. *(Bonus)* **Event-study plot** — Instead of a single post dummy, create period-specific interaction terms between `union` and each value of `t` (omitting `t == 3` as the reference period). Estimate a regression of `lwage` on these interactions plus `ed`, `blk`, `fem`, `south`, and time dummies. Use `coefplot` (if installed) or manually inspect the coefficients to assess whether pre-trends are present before your chosen cutoff.

- **Hint 2 (Task 3)**: You can compute cell means with `summarize lwage if union == 1 & post == 0`, repeating for each of the four cells. The DiD estimate is (union_post − union_pre) − (nonunion_post − nonunion_pre).
- **Hint 3 (Task 4)**: To create the interaction term you can use `gen union_post = union * post`. In `xtreg, fe` the main effects of time-invariant variables like `blk` and `fem` will be absorbed — Stata will warn you about this.
