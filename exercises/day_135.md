# Day 135: Instrumental Variables with Labor Supply Data

## Background
The `laborsup` dataset contains 500 observations on female labor supply decisions, including women's income, education, husband's education, number of children, and other household characteristics. Instrumental variables (IV) estimation is essential in labor economics when a regressor (such as education or income) is endogenous — correlated with the error term — and a valid instrument must be both relevant and exogenous.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the data** — Load the dataset and run `describe` and `summarize`. Identify which variable could plausibly serve as the outcome (`fem_inc`), which is the suspected endogenous regressor (`fem_work`), and which variables might serve as controls or instruments. Note the range and type of each variable.

2. **OLS baseline** — Regress `fem_inc` on `fem_work`, `kids`, `other_inc`, and `fem_educ` using OLS. Interpret the coefficient on `fem_work`: what does it suggest about the relationship between a woman working and her income? Save the OLS coefficient on `fem_work` for comparison in later tasks.

3. **First-stage regression** — Propose `male_educ` as an instrument for `fem_work`. Run the first-stage OLS regression of `fem_work` on `male_educ`, `kids`, `other_inc`, and `fem_educ`. Report the coefficient on `male_educ`, its standard error, and its t-statistic. Does `male_educ` appear to be a relevant instrument based on this regression?

4. **IV estimation with `ivregress`** — Estimate the structural equation using two-stage least squares (2SLS): regress `fem_inc` on `fem_work` (instrumented by `male_educ`), `kids`, `other_inc`, and `fem_educ`. Compare the IV coefficient on `fem_work` to the OLS estimate from Task 2 and comment on the direction and magnitude of any difference.

5. *(Bonus)* **Test instrument validity** — After your `ivregress 2sls` estimation, use `estat firststage` to retrieve the first-stage F-statistic. Does the instrument pass the conventional threshold (F > 10) for instrument relevance? Then use `estat endogenous` to run a Durbin–Wu–Hausman test for endogeneity of `fem_work`. Interpret both test results in the context of whether IV was necessary.
