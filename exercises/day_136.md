## Background
The Mroz (1987) PSID dataset contains information on married women's labor force participation, wages, education, and family characteristics — a classic resource for studying female labor supply. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential when a regressor like education is endogenous (correlated with unobserved ability), and this dataset provides natural candidate instruments in the form of parental education variables.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the key variables** — Load the dataset and summarize `wwage76`, `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `wmedyrs`, and `wfedyrs`. Report the mean and standard deviation of each. Note which variables could plausibly serve as instruments for wife's education and briefly explain in a comment why parental education might satisfy the exclusion restriction.

2. **Run an OLS baseline** — Regress `wwage76` (wife's hourly wage in 1976) on `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `smsa`, and `kl6`. Store the OLS coefficient on `wedyrs` and its standard error. Note in a comment whether you would expect OLS to overestimate or underestimate the true return to education if ability is positively correlated with both wage and schooling.

3. **First-stage regression** — Regress `wedyrs` on `wmedyrs`, `wfedyrs`, `wexper`, `smsa`, and `kl6`. Report the F-statistic for joint significance of the two instruments (`wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs`) using `test wmedyrs wfedyrs`. Comment on whether the instruments appear strong (conventional threshold: F > 10).

4. **IV estimation with `ivregress`** — Estimate the wage equation using 2SLS: regress `wwage76` on `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `smsa`, and `kl6`, instrumenting `wedyrs` with `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs`. Compare the IV coefficient on `wedyrs` to your OLS estimate from Task 2. Then run the Hausman-style endogeneity test using `estat endogenous` and comment on whether OLS appears inconsistent.

5. *(Bonus)* **Overidentification test** — Since you have two instruments for one endogenous variable, you can test overidentifying restrictions. After your `ivregress 2sls` command, run `estat overid`. Report the Sargan–Hansen statistic and its p-value, and explain in a comment what it would mean if the null hypothesis were rejected.
