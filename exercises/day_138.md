## Background
The Mroz 1987 PSID dataset contains detailed labor supply information for married women, including wages, hours worked, education, and family characteristics. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential in labor economics when a key regressor — such as education — is suspected to be endogenous due to omitted ability bias or measurement error.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the data** — Load the dataset and summarize the variables `wwage76`, `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, and `smsa`. Note the ranges carefully, then restrict the sample to women who participated in the labor force (`lfp == 1`) for all subsequent tasks.

2. **Run a naive OLS regression** — Regress `wwage76` (wife's 1976 hourly wage) on `wedyrs` (wife's years of education), `wexper` (wife's work experience), `kl6` (kids under 6), and `smsa` using `regress`. Record the coefficient on `wedyrs` and its standard error.

3. **Estimate the first stage** — Instrument `wedyrs` using `wmedyrs` (wife's mother's years of education) and `wfedyrs` (wife's father's years of education) as instruments. Run a regression of `wedyrs` on these two instruments plus the exogenous controls (`wexper`, `kl6`, `smsa`). Test joint significance of the instruments using `testparms` and comment on instrument relevance.

4. **Run the IV (2SLS) regression** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to estimate the effect of `wedyrs` on `wwage76`, instrumenting for `wedyrs` with `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs`, while controlling for `wexper`, `kl6`, and `smsa`. Compare the IV coefficient on `wedyrs` to your OLS estimate. Then run the Hausman endogeneity test using `estat endogenous` and interpret the result.

5. *(Bonus)* **Overidentification test** — After the `ivregress` command, run `estat overid` to perform the Sargan–Hansen test of overidentifying restrictions. State the null hypothesis of the test, report the p-value, and discuss what it implies about the validity of your instruments.

- **Hint 1 (Task 3)**: After regressing `wedyrs` on the instruments and controls, you can test joint significance of both instruments simultaneously using `test wmedyrs wfedyrs` — a rule of thumb for relevance is an F-statistic above 10.

- **Hint 2 (Task 4)**: The syntax for `ivregress 2sls` places endogenous regressors and their instruments in parentheses: `ivregress 2sls depvar exog_controls (endog_var = instruments)`. After estimation, `estat endogenous` tests whether the OLS and IV estimates differ significantly, with rejection suggesting endogeneity is a real concern.
