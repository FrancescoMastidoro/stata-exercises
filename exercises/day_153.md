## Background
The Mroz 1987 PSID dataset contains information on married women's labor supply, wages, education, and family background — a classic testbed for IV estimation in labor economics. IV methods are essential when a regressor (such as education) is endogenous, meaning it is correlated with unobserved determinants of the outcome (such as ability), so that OLS estimates are biased.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the key variables** — Load the dataset and summarize `wwage76`, `wedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, and `nwinc`. Report the means and standard deviations. Based on the variable ranges in the codebook, which variable has the most variation relative to its mean?

2. **Estimate an OLS benchmark** — Regress `wwage76` (wife's 1976 hourly wage) on `wedyrs` (wife's education), `wexper` (experience), `kl6` (kids under 6), and `nwinc` (non-wife income). Note the coefficient on `wedyrs` and its standard error. Remember that `wwage76` includes zeros for non-employed women — keep them in the sample for now.

3. **Run a 2SLS regression using `ivregress`** — Use `wmedyrs` (wife's mother's education) and `wfedyrs` (wife's father's education) as instruments for `wedyrs`. Keep the same controls (`wexper`, `kl6`, `nwinc`) as exogenous regressors. Use the `2sls` estimator. Compare the coefficient on `wedyrs` to your OLS estimate from Task 2 — does it increase or decrease, and by how much?

4. **Test instrument relevance** — After the `ivregress` command, run `estat firststage` to examine the first-stage F-statistic. Then manually run the first-stage regression of `wedyrs` on `wmedyrs`, `wfedyrs`, `wexper`, `kl6`, and `nwinc` using plain `regress`. Report whether both instruments are individually significant at the 5% level and whether the first-stage F exceeds the conventional threshold of 10. Discuss briefly in a comment whether your instruments appear strong or weak.

5. *(Bonus)* **Test overidentifying restrictions** — After re-running the `ivregress 2sls` model, use `estat overid` to conduct a Sargan–Hansen test of overidentifying restrictions. State the null hypothesis of this test, report the p-value from your output, and explain in a Stata comment (`//`) what you would conclude about the joint validity of `wmedyrs` and `wfedyrs` as instruments.
