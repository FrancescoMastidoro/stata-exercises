# Day 174: Instrumental Variables (IV) with Labor Supply Data

**Dataset**: `webuse laborsup`
**Estimated time**: ~25 minutes

## Background
The `laborsup` dataset contains 500 observations on female labor supply, recording women's income, their husbands' education, number of children, and other household variables. Instrumental variables estimation is a cornerstone technique in labor economics for addressing endogeneity — for example, when a regressor like `fem_educ` may be correlated with unobserved ability or motivation that also drives earnings.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the data** — Load the dataset and use `describe` and `summarize` to familiarize yourself with all six variables. Note the range of `fem_educ` (8–16), `male_educ` (8–16), `other_inc`, `kids`, `fem_inc`, and `fem_work`. Produce a correlation matrix for all numeric variables using `correlate`.

2. **Run an OLS baseline** — Regress `fem_inc` on `fem_educ`, `kids`, and `other_inc`. Interpret the coefficient on `fem_educ`: what does it say about the relationship between a woman's education and her income in this sample?

3. **Run a first-stage regression** — Regress `fem_educ` on the proposed instrument `male_educ`, plus the controls `kids` and `other_inc`. Check whether `male_educ` is a strong predictor of `fem_educ` by examining its t-statistic and the overall F-statistic of the regression. A weak instrument will produce unreliable IV estimates.

4. **Estimate the IV model** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to instrument `fem_educ` with `male_educ`, controlling for `kids` and `other_inc`, with `fem_inc` as the outcome. Compare the IV coefficient on `fem_educ` to the OLS estimate from Task 2. Then run `estat firststage` to retrieve the first-stage F-statistic and assess instrument strength against the conventional threshold of 10.

5. *(Bonus)* **Test for endogeneity and run a robustness check** — After your `ivregress` estimation, use `estat endogenous` to formally test whether `fem_educ` is endogenous (i.e., whether IV is actually needed over OLS). Then re-run the IV model replacing `ivregress 2sls` with `ivregress liml` and compare the LIML coefficient on `fem_educ` to the 2SLS result. Under what conditions would LIML be preferred?

- **Hint 1 (Task 3)**: The first-stage F-statistic appears at the bottom of the `regress` output; you can also retrieve it from `e(F)` after estimation. The rule of thumb is F > 10 for a strong instrument.
- **Hint 2 (Task 4)**: The syntax for `ivregress 2sls` places the endogenous regressor and its instrument inside parentheses: `ivregress 2sls depvar controls (endogenous = instrument)`. Run `estat firststage` immediately after to see the first-stage diagnostics.
