## Background
The `401k` dataset contains firm-level information on 401(k) pension plan participation, including plan age, match rates, employment size, and participation rates. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential in labor economics when explanatory variables are endogenous — here, the match rate (`mrate`) may be correlated with unobserved firm-level generosity, making OLS estimates of its effect on participation biased.

## Your tasks

1. **Explore the key variables** — Load the dataset and summarize `prate`, `mrate`, `age`, `sole`, and `ltotemp`. Note the range of `mrate` (0 to 2) and `prate` (near 0 to 1). Report the mean of `prate` and comment on whether most firms have high participation rates.

2. **Estimate an OLS benchmark** — Regress `prate` on `mrate`, `age`, `agesq`, `ltotemp`, `ltotempsq`, and `sole`. Interpret the coefficient on `mrate`: what does OLS suggest about the effect of employer matching on participation?

3. **Run a 2SLS regression using `ivregress`** — Instrument `mrate` with `sole` (an indicator for whether the 401k is the firm's *sole* retirement plan). Use `ivregress 2sls` with the same control variables (`age`, `agesq`, `ltotemp`, `ltotempsq`) but treat `mrate` as endogenous and `sole` as the instrument. Compare the IV estimate on `mrate` to the OLS estimate from Task 2.

4. **Test instrument relevance and run the first-stage diagnostics** — After `ivregress`, run `estat firststage` to examine the first-stage F-statistic. Then separately estimate the first-stage regression manually (regress `mrate` on `sole`, `age`, `agesq`, `ltotemp`, `ltotempsq`) and check whether `sole` is a statistically significant predictor of `mrate`. Discuss whether the instrument appears relevant.

5. *(Bonus)* **Test for endogeneity and overidentification** — Re-estimate the IV model adding `ltotemp` interacted with `sole` as a second instrument (create the interaction with `gen sole_ltotemp = sole * ltotemp`). Run `ivregress 2sls` with both `sole` and `sole_ltotemp` as instruments, then execute `estat overid` to perform a Sargan–Hansen overidentification test. Interpret the p-value: do the instruments appear valid?
