## Background
The Mroz (1987) dataset contains information on 753 married women from the PSID, covering labor supply decisions, wages, education, and family characteristics. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential in labor economics when a regressor like education is likely correlated with unobserved ability, and a valid instrument can isolate exogenous variation in that regressor.

## Your tasks

1. **OLS benchmark** — Load the dataset and estimate a simple OLS regression of `wwage76` (wife's 1976 hourly wage) on `wedyrs` (wife's years of education) and `wexper` (wife's years of experience). Report and briefly comment on the coefficient on `wedyrs`.

2. **Assess the instrument** — Wife's mother's education (`wmedyrs`) is a candidate instrument for `wedyrs`. Run a regression of `wedyrs` on `wmedyrs`, `wexper`, and `unemprt` (local unemployment rate). Check whether `wmedyrs` is a strong predictor of `wedyrs` by inspecting the t-statistic and F-statistic of the first-stage regression.

3. **IV estimation** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to estimate the effect of `wedyrs` on `wwage76`, instrumenting `wedyrs` with `wmedyrs` and controlling for `wexper` and `unemprt`. Store the results and compare the IV coefficient on `wedyrs` to the OLS coefficient from Task 1.

4. **Test instrument validity** — After the `ivregress` command, run `estat endogenous` to test whether `wedyrs` is actually endogenous (i.e., whether IV was necessary), and then run `estat overid` — but first explain in a comment why this test cannot be performed in the just-identified case (one instrument, one endogenous regressor). Add a second instrument `wfedyrs` (wife's father's years of education) and re-estimate with both instruments, then run `estat overid` to obtain the Sargan–Hansen test.

5. *(Bonus)* **LIML alternative** — Re-estimate the model from Task 4 (two instruments) using the Limited Information Maximum Likelihood estimator (`ivregress liml`) instead of 2SLS. Compare the LIML and 2SLS coefficients on `wedyrs`. In a comment, explain in which situations LIML is preferred over 2SLS.

## Hints

- **Hint 1 (Task 2)**: After `regress wedyrs wmedyrs wexper unemprt`, type `estat summarize` or simply look at the F-statistic reported in the ANOVA table at the top of the output; a common rule of thumb for instrument strength is F > 10.

- **Hint 2 (Task 3)**: The syntax for `ivregress 2sls` puts the endogenous regressor and its instrument(s) inside parentheses: `ivregress 2sls depvar controls (endogvar = instruments)`.

- **Hint 3 (Task 4)**: The `estat endogenous` and `estat overid` post-estimation commands must be typed immediately after `ivregress`. Remember: the overidentification test requires at least as many instruments as endogenous regressors *plus one* — with exactly one instrument and one endogenous variable the model is just-identified and the test has zero degrees of freedom.

- **Hint 4 (Task 5)**: To switch from 2SLS to LIML, simply replace `2sls` with `liml` in the `ivregress` command; all other syntax stays the same.
