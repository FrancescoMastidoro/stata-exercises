## Background
The `regsmpl` dataset is a panel of NLS women observed across multiple years (1968–1988), containing wages, education, labor market experience, and geographic indicators. Difference-in-differences (DiD) is a cornerstone method in labor economics for estimating causal effects when random assignment is impossible, exploiting variation in treatment timing across groups and time.

## Your tasks

1. **Load and inspect** — Load the dataset with `webuse regsmpl`. Identify the panel structure (individual and time identifiers), declare it using `xtset`, and summarize `ln_wage`, `south`, `black`, and `year`. Report how many unique individuals and years are in the data.

2. **Create a post-period indicator** — Define a binary variable called `post` equal to 1 if `year >= 78` (i.e., 1978 onward) and 0 otherwise. Then tabulate `post` against `year` to verify the cutoff is correct. This will serve as your "after" indicator in a DiD design.

3. **Construct the DiD interaction term** — Create a variable called `did` equal to `black * post`. This interaction captures the differential change in log wages for Black women after 1978 relative to non-Black women. Tabulate `black` and `post` jointly to confirm the four cells of the 2×2 DiD table are populated.

4. **Estimate and interpret the DiD regression** — Regress `ln_wage` on `black`, `post`, and `did`, controlling for `ttl_exp`, `south`, and `grade`. Include individual fixed effects by adding `i.idcode` — but since this would be computationally heavy with 28,534 obs, instead use the `xtreg` command with the `fe` option (after `xtset`) and include `post`, `did`, `ttl_exp`, `south`, and `grade` as regressors. Note which variables drop out due to collinearity with fixed effects and explain why. Report and interpret the coefficient on `did`.

5. *(Bonus)* **Event-study plot** — Instead of a single `post` dummy, create year-specific interaction terms `black_yYY = black * (year == YY)` for each year in the data using a loop (omitting one pre-treatment year as the baseline). Regress `ln_wage` on these interactions plus individual fixed effects (`xtreg, fe`) and `ttl_exp` and `grade`. Then use `coefplot` (if installed) or manually `matrix`-extract the coefficients and confidence intervals to sketch whether pre-trends are flat before 1978 — the key parallel-trends check.

The `did` coefficient is identified because `post` varies over time and interacts with the group indicator.
