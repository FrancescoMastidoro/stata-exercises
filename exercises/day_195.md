## Background
The Mroz (1987) PSID dataset contains information on married women's labor supply, wages, education, and family characteristics — a classic resource for labor economics. Instrumental Variables (IV) estimation is essential when a regressor is endogenous (correlated with the error term), and this dataset provides natural candidates for instruments such as parental education variables.

## Your tasks

1. **OLS benchmark** — Run an OLS regression of `lwage` on `wedyrs` (wife's education in years) and `wexper` (wife's experience). Report the coefficient on `wedyrs` and note its interpretation carefully.

2. **Instrument validity check** — Before running IV, assess whether `wfedyrs` (wife's father's years of education) and `wmedyrs` (wife's mother's years of education) are plausible instruments for `wedyrs`. Run a regression of `wedyrs` on `wfedyrs`, `wmedyrs`, and `wexper`, and test their joint significance using an F-test. Comment on whether the instruments appear relevant.

3. **Two-Stage Least Squares (2SLS)** — Use `ivregress 2sls` to estimate the return to education (`wedyrs`) on `lwage`, treating `wedyrs` as endogenous, instrumenting with `wfedyrs` and `wmedyrs`, and including `wexper` as an exogenous control. Report the IV coefficient on `wedyrs` and compare it to the OLS estimate from Task 1.

4. **Test for endogeneity** — After the `ivregress` command, run `estat endogenous` to formally test whether `wedyrs` is endogenous. Interpret the test statistic and p-value: does the test suggest OLS is inconsistent, or is the endogeneity concern mild in this sample?

5. *(Bonus)* **Overidentification test** — Since you have two instruments for one endogenous variable, the model is overidentified. Run `estat overid` after the 2SLS regression to perform the Sargan–Hansen test. Interpret the result: do the instruments appear to satisfy the exclusion restriction jointly? Then re-estimate using `ivregress liml` instead of `2sls` and compare the coefficient on `wedyrs` — under what conditions would LIML be preferred?

## Hints

- **Hint 1 (Task 3)**: In `ivregress 2sls`, the syntax separates endogenous regressors from instruments inside parentheses: `ivregress 2sls depvar exog_controls (endog_var = instruments)`. Think carefully about which variables belong inside the parentheses and which go outside.

- **Hint 2 (Task 2)**: A useful rule of thumb for instrument relevance is that the first-stage F-statistic on the excluded instruments should exceed 10. Use `test wfedyrs wmedyrs` immediately after the first-stage regression to retrieve this joint F-statistic.

- **Hint 3 (Task 4)**: `estat endogenous` must be run immediately after `ivregress` and tests the null hypothesis that the suspected endogenous regressor can actually be treated as exogenous. A small p-value is evidence *against* OLS consistency.

- **Hint 4 (Bonus)**: LIML (Limited Information Maximum Likelihood) is more robust than 2SLS when instruments are weak. After re-estimating with `ivregress liml`, use `estimates store` to hold both sets of results and compare coefficients side by side with `estimates table`.
